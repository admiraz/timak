About Us

Engineering Specialized Vehicles Since 2016

TIMAK combines advanced engineering, skilled manufacturing, and international standards to deliver custom vehicle solutions for critical operations worldwide.

Years in Industry

Discover Our Story

We are TIMAK, an Albanian company established in 2016, specializing in the production and integration of superstructures for functional and custom vehicles that serve key sectors such as emergency services, energy, defense, and public works. With a presence in over 20 countries, we design and manufacture ambulances, fire trucks, pile drivers, military and armored vehicles, and a wide range of other specialized solutions tailored to our clients' needs. We believe in the power of local manufacturing and the importance of empowering women in male-dominated industries — values embedded in our leadership and company culture.

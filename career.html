Civil Engineer

Key Responsibilities:

  • Design & Analysis: Develop and review structural designs and metal constructions for special vehicles.
  • Supervision: Monitor production line implementation for compliance with engineering designs.
  • Coordination: Collaborate with production and procurement teams on technical material specifications.
  • Documentation: Prepare technical reports, cost estimates, and project documentation.

Requirements:

  • University degree in Civil Engineering (Structural Engineering specialization a plus).
  • Strong AutoCAD and SAP2000 (or similar) knowledge.
  • Previous experience in structural design or industrial project supervision.
  • Analytical, organized, with strong problem-solving skills.
  • Professional qualification certificate (DAP).

Warehouse Manager

Key Responsibilities:

  • Stock Management: Receive, inspect, and organize materials and spare parts.
  • Production Supply: Prepare and issue materials to technicians and engineers per production needs.
  • Inventory Control: Conduct periodic inventory checks and maintain accurate stock records.
  • Organization: Maintain warehouse cleanliness and order for efficient workflow.

Requirements:

  • Previous experience as a warehouse assistant (technical/industrial warehouse preferred).
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to item codes and details.
  • Basic computer skills.
  • Reliability, integrity, and teamwork.

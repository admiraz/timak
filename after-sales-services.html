After Sales Services

At TIMAK, our commitment doesn't end with delivery — it begins a new chapter. We stand by the quality of our products and the satisfaction of our clients, offering a reliable and responsive after-sales support system to ensure the long-term performance of your investment.

How We Support You

Warranty, technical support, and training — long after delivery

Warranty Services

All TIMAK vehicles and equipment come with a standard warranty. In the case of technical issues, our team will diagnose and resolve problems efficiently, either remotely or on-site when necessary.

Technical Support

Our engineers and technicians are available for guidance, troubleshooting, and on-site visits when needed. Availability of technical support services depends on the project scope or client agreement.

Training & Manuals

Comprehensive training is offered upon delivery, and digital or printed manuals are provided for all equipment. Additional training sessions or custom training programs are available upon request and subject to client requirements.
